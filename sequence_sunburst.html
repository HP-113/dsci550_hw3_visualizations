Description

This sunburst chart visualizes the hierarchical structure of haunted reports by time of day, crime event type, and apparition type. The innermost ring represents the time of day when the event occurred. The middle layer displays the associated type of crime or incident, and the outer ring breaks down the types of apparitions involved. Hovering over any segment reveals its percentage share and total number of mentions.

Insight

One key insight from the sunburst chart is that a significant portion of haunted reports cluster around certain times of day—particularly the evening—often associated with specific types of crimes and apparition phenomena. The structure also reveals that some crime categories lead to a wider variety of apparition types than others, suggesting a potential link between perceived paranormal activity and the nature of the incident. Additionally, the prominence of "unknown" categories in some branches highlights data uncertainty or incomplete records.
