Description

This map visualizes haunted location reports across the United States. Blue circles indicate cities with reported haunted places, and larger circles represent more frequent reports.

Insight

The distribution reveals heavy clustering of haunted place reports along the East Coast and parts of California and Texas. These clusters may reflect historical settlement patterns, cultural folklore, or population density and reporting accessibility.
