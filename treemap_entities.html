Description

The treemap visualizes the most frequently occurring keywords and phrases from the haunted places dataset. It uses tokenized and cleaned textual data (lowercased, stopword-removed) from narrative descriptions to display word frequency as proportional rectangles. Each box's size corresponds to how often a phrase appeared across all entries, offering an at-a-glance analysis of the most dominant themes in ghost stories.

Insight

The overwhelming presence of the word “night” and closely associated phrases such as “late at night” “midnight”, and “the night” reinforces the clear temporal pattern in haunting narratives. This visual confirms earlier NLP-based observations from Assignment 1—that over 60% of supernatural sightings were reported during nighttime hours (9PM–3AM). In addition, terms like “Civil War,” “Native American,” “WWII,” “Indians,” and “Union” reveal deep historical embedding within ghost stories. These keyword clusters suggest that hauntings are not randomly imagined but often tied to periods of trauma, displacement, and unresolved cultural memory. This aligns well with folkloric studies on ghost narratives being used to process generational grief or guilt.
